Privacy Policy for Sharpwit

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Creative Solution Team (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Sharpwit: Daily Brain Games mobile app (the “App”).

Information we collect

Sharpwit does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information.

Your game progress (coins, scores, streaks, purchases made with coins and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information, and
  • ad interaction data (for example, ad views and taps).

This collection is performed by Google, not by us. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases

The App offers an optional one-time “Remove ads” purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google Payments’ Privacy Notice. We never receive your card or payment details. The App only asks Google Play whether the purchase has been made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App schedules a notification on your device. Reminders are created and stored on your device only; no information is sent to us.

Sharing and feedback

When you tap Share, the App opens your phone’s share menu with a short text about your result. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

When you tap Send feedback, the App opens your email app with a message to us that includes the App version, Android version and phone model to help us fix problems. You can edit or delete this before sending. We use emails you send only to reply to you and improve the App.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We do not operate servers that store your data. Data handled by Google AdMob and Google Play is protected under Google’s security practices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
